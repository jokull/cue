Cue Voice

  • What it is: the script follows your voice as you read, and your takes get captions timed to your words. Without it, Cue is a prompter you scroll by hand.
  • Price and renewal: Cue Voice is a yearly auto-renewing subscription, with a free week for new subscribers. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm (or when the free week ends) and renews each year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Cancelling: manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from Cue's menu. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Cue Voice until the period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • Fair use: Cue Voice covers generous everyday use — around a few hundred clips and three hours of finished takes a month. Past that, voice tracking and captions pause until the next month.
  • Languages: recognition quality varies by language and recording conditions; captions can be edited before you export.
