Recording

Hold the red button until its ring fills, then speak. Let go to end the clip. Slide up while holding to keep recording hands-free; tap to stop. Tap ✓ when your take is done.

The script doesn't follow me

Following your voice comes with Cue Voice, and needs an internet connection and your permission to send audio for transcription. Without the subscription you scroll the script yourself (the tag in the top corner says MANUAL); subscribers who are offline or haven't given permission get a steady auto-scroll. The tag shows VOICE when it's following you. Tap the tag to switch.

Cue Voice subscription

$29.99 a year after a free week; it adds the voice-following script and word-timed captions. Manage or cancel it from Cue's menu (☰ → Manage Cue Voice) or in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Restore a purchase from the Cue Voice screen. See the terms.

Moving through the script

Between clips, drag anywhere on the camera image to scroll the script; your next clip starts at the highlighted line. In manual mode the same drag scrolls while you record — lock the recording (slide up) to free your thumb.

Fixing a clip

Tap a clip to select it, then hold to record it again. To delete, tap the backspace button twice; Undo appears for a few seconds.

Captions

Captions are timed to your words after you finish a take. Tap Edit words to fix a word or its timing, and pick Fun, Pro or Serif. Export with captions burned in, without them, or as .srt and .vtt subtitle files.
