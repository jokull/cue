Cue

A teleprompter that follows your voice, and captions that are done when you stop.

  • Read naturally. Your script sits right under the camera and moves line by line as you speak.
  • Record in clips. Hold to record, let go to pause. Retake, reorder or delete clips before you finish.
  • Captions in three styles. Every word timed to how you said it, burned into your video or exported as subtitles.
  • Works in Icelandic, English and many more languages.
